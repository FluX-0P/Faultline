# B5: Retries a timed-out charge without an idempotency key

- **Switched on by:** `payments.provider.idempotency-key: none`. The bug-free value is `payment-id`.
- **Scan pattern:** P5, flagged at its location.
- **Code:** [ProviderClient.java](../../demo-app/src/main/java/dev/faultline/demo/payment/ProviderClient.java)
- **Plan:** [b5.plan.yaml](../../benchmark/b5.plan.yaml)

## The bug

A charge that timed out may still have gone through. The worker tries it again with nothing that tells the
provider it is the same charge:

```java
if (idempotencyKey) request = request.header("Idempotency-Key", p.paymentId());
```

## How Faultline proves it

The fake provider charges every 100th charge, then replies 3s later, after the worker's 1s timeout, 5 times;
2,000 payments.

The check that fails is `one-charge-per-payment-at-the-provider`.

## Evidence

| Run | Fault fired | What the check found |
|---|---|---|
| run-1 | 5 of 5 late replies | 5 payments charged twice at the provider: pay-42-171, -222, -67 ... |
| run-2 | 5 of 5 late replies | 5 payments charged twice: pay-42-330, -118, -220 ... |
| baseline | none | one charge per payment at the provider |

Confirmed: the check failed in 2 of 2 runs with the fault, and passed in the baseline. On the bug-free build, the same plan came out suspected: the fault fired in both runs and every check passed.

## The fix

Send the payment ID as the `Idempotency-Key` (`payments.provider.idempotency-key: payment-id`). The provider then
returns the first charge instead of making a second.

Rerun: `./faultline benchmark --bug B5`
