# B2: Auto-commit with a thread pool

- **Switched on by:** `payments.processing: thread-pool`. The bug-free value is `listener-thread`.
- **Scan pattern:** P2, flagged at its location.
- **Code:** [PaymentListener.java](../../demo-app/src/main/java/dev/faultline/demo/payment/PaymentListener.java)
- **Plan:** [b2.plan.yaml](../../benchmark/b2.plan.yaml)

## The bug

The listener hands each payment to a thread pool and acknowledges its message at once, before the payment is
done.

```java
pool.submit(() -> service.process(event));
ack.acknowledge();   // acknowledged as soon as it is handed over
```

A crash loses what the pool had not finished. Kafka never delivers those messages again, because they were
acknowledged.

## How Faultline proves it

Crash worker-1 the moment `PaymentListener#onMessage` returns, on every 150th message, twice; 3,000 payments at 100/s.

The check that fails is `no-lost`.

## Evidence

| Run | Fault fired | What the check found |
|---|---|---|
| run-1 | 2 of 2 crashes, 2 restarts | 2 payments lost: pay-42-519, pay-42-2057 |
| run-2 | 2 of 2 crashes, 2 restarts | 2 payments lost: pay-42-305, pay-42-1801 |
| baseline | none | no-lost passed: all 3,000 applied |

Confirmed: the check failed in 2 of 2 runs with the fault, and passed in the baseline. On the bug-free build, the same plan came out suspected: the fault fired in both runs and every check passed.

## The fix

Process on the listener's own thread, and acknowledge once the payment has committed
(`payments.processing: listener-thread`).

Rerun: `./faultline benchmark --bug B2`
